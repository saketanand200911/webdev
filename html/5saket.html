USEFUL INFO:

  1. Quantum Computing
    2. Quantum computing uses the principles of quantum mechanics to process information. Unlike traditional computers that use bits (0 or 1), quantum computers use qubits, which can represent multiple states. It has potential applications in cryptography, drug discovery, optimization, and scientific research.
  2. Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI)
    3. BCI technology creates a direct communication pathway between the brain and a computer. It can interpret brain signals and translate them into commands, allowing users to control devices without physical movement. This technology could be especially useful for assistive devices, medical treatments, and human-computer interaction.
  3. Edge Computing
    4. Edge computing processes data closer to where it is generated instead of sending everything to a distant cloud server. This reduces latency and can improve speed, reliability, and privacy. It is widely useful in self-driving vehicles, smart cities, IoT devices, and industrial automation.
